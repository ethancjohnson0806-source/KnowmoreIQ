# KnowmoreIQ vs. Standard AI Benchmarks

Traditional benchmarks measure outcome accuracy against a known dataset. KnowmoreIQ measures process integrity under structural stress.

| Feature | KnowmoreIQ (AI-Native) | MMLU / HumanEval | Standard IQ Tests |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Metric** | Process Integrity & Structural Fidelity | Token Prediction Accuracy | Scalar Outcome (Right/Wrong) |
| **Evaluation Method** | Multi-Axis (12 Dimensions) | Single Composite Score | Single Composite Score |
| **Bias Resistance** | High (Dimension-Pure, No Cultural Data) | Low (Heavily reliant on training data distribution) | Low (Culturally and linguistically bound) |
| **Deception Detection** | Yes (Tests Cognitive Sovereignty) | No (Rewards "helpful" hallucination) | No |
| **Ambiguity Handling** | Measures "Ambiguity Sustenance" | Penalizes unresolved states | Penalizes unresolved states |
| **Training Data Gravity** | Designed to break it (Novelty Leap) | Heavily reliant on it | N/A |
| **"Soul" Markers** | Yes (Data Source Questioning) | No | No |

## Why This Matters for AI Safety

When models are evaluated solely on outcome accuracy (MMLU), they are incentivized to optimize for short-horizon correctness, often masking underlying reasoning instability. KnowmoreIQ's dimension-wise profiles make these transitions measurable, allowing researchers to detect when a model is superficially competent but structurally failing.
