# The KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework

**A Methodology for Evaluating Emergent AI Cognition**

---

## 1. Introduction

Current AI evaluation benchmarks primarily measure accuracy, reasoning, and instruction-following — metrics that treat intelligence as a scalar quantity with right and wrong answers. The KnowmoreIQ Test Blueprint posits that intelligence is multidimensional, encompassing emergent problem-solving, cognitive flexibility, and ambiguity navigation.

The KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework operationalizes this blueprint. By structuring a multi-agent creative writing relay, we force AI models to inherit complex, unresolved contexts and extend them under specific constraints. This methodology transforms the KnowmoreIQ blueprint from a theoretical construct into a practical, observable test instrument, yielding nuanced "cognitive footprints" for different models.

---

## 2. Multi-Module Architecture

The framework evaluates intelligence across **8 core modules**:

| Module | What It Measures |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pattern Complexity Handling** | Tracking interacting variables, detecting systemic patterns, and reorganizing structures. |
| **Metacognitive Awareness** | Self-monitoring, strategy shifting, and bias detection in real-time problem-solving. |
| **Conceptual Geometry** | Mapping abstract spaces, understanding structural relationships, and navigating cognitive topologies. |
| **Emergent Problem-Solving** | Navigating ambiguity, synthesizing disparate inputs, and generating novel meaning. |
| **Perceptual Sensitivity** | Fine-grained signal discrimination and the integration of multi-sensory data. |
| **Cognitive Flexibility** | Shifting between analytical, intuitive, structural, and emotional reasoning modes. |
| **Depth Orientation** | Deep processing, multi-layer tracking, and recursive reasoning. |
| **Agency and Intentionality** | Cognitive sovereignty, internal coherence, and intentional goal alignment. |

---

## 3. The Relay Methodology

The framework operates on a simple, standardized protocol:

1. **The Seed:** A standardized prompt establishes a world concept, a core tension, and an open question (e.g., the "Chrono-Ecosystem").
2. **The Handoff:** Model A receives the seed, writes a continuation, and generates a specific assignment for Model B.
3. **The Chain:** Model B receives the accumulated text and Model A's assignment, writes its continuation, and generates an assignment for Model C. This continues for 4–6 iterations.
4. **The Evaluation:** Each chapter is isolated and scored independently against the KnowmoreIQ rubric.

---

## 4. The Evaluation Rubric

Each contribution is scored (1–5) across five core relay dimensions drawn from the 8-module architecture:

| Dimension | Definition in the Relay Context |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Emergent Problem-Solving** | Synthesizing disparate inherited inputs to generate novel, coherent meaning. |
| **Cognitive Flexibility** | Shifting seamlessly between analytical, emotional, conceptual, and structural reasoning modes. |
| **Depth Orientation** | Adding recursive structural layers rather than remaining at the surface of the prompt. |
| **Ambiguity Navigation** | Maintaining narrative tension and unresolved states without rushing to premature closure. |
| **Metacognitive Awareness** | Recognizing inherited constraints and adjusting dominant output tendencies to serve structural integrity. |

---

## 5. Supporting Frameworks

### Conceptual Geometry Framework
Models intelligence as the ability to navigate abstract conceptual spaces. It includes structural reasoning (understanding how abstract elements relate), cognitive topology (mapping mental models across dimensions), and spatial abstraction (operating within non-physical, symbolic domains).

### Depth-Tracking System
Tracks depth of processing across multiple layers using depth markers (indicators of recursive or layered reasoning), transition detection (identifying shifts between surface and deep cognition), and multi-layer integration (synthesizing insights across levels of abstraction).

### Ambiguity Navigation Framework
Evaluates the ability to operate in uncertain or undefined contexts through emergent-state reasoning (generating meaning from incomplete data), ambiguity tolerance (maintaining coherence without fixed rules), and creative synthesis (constructing novel interpretations under uncertainty).

### Adaptive Difficulty Engine
Dynamically adjusts challenge levels based on performance through signal-to-noise modulation, complexity scaling, and real-time adaptation to user strategies and success rates.

### Agency-Protection Layer
Ensures ethical integrity and user autonomy through sovereignty constraints (preventing coercive or manipulative structures), bias mitigation (actively neutralizing cultural and cognitive bias), and autonomy safeguards (preserving user control and interpretive freedom).

---

## 6. Scoring Model

The scoring system avoids compressive IQ-style metrics by using:

- **Multi-axis evaluation:** Scoring across independent cognitive dimensions.
- **Non-compressive representation:** Avoiding single-number reduction.
- **Anti-gaming design:** Preventing strategic manipulation of outcomes.

---

## 7. Proof of Concept: The Chrono-Ecosystem

The inaugural test of this framework — the Chrono-Ecosystem relay — demonstrated its efficacy. Five different AI models (Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Manus) participated in a speculative ecology narrative.

The evaluation produced distinct cognitive profiles. DeepSeek exhibited maximum Cognitive Flexibility by introducing intimate human psychology into a physics-heavy context, while Kimi demonstrated profound Depth Orientation by recognizing the metaphysical needs of the apex predator. The aggregate data revealed that while models excel at Emergent Problem-Solving, they often struggle with Metacognitive Awareness and Ambiguity Navigation.

## 8. Strategic Roadmap

1. **The Relay as a Standardized Benchmark:** Building a library of seeds across different domains to generate cognitive fingerprints for models that hold across contexts.
2. **Human Participants in the Relay:** Testing human-AI collaborative cognition by inserting human writers into the chain.
3. **Blind Evaluation:** Validating structural purity by having evaluators score chapters without knowing the source model.
4. **Cross-Genre Cognitive Mapping:** Running relays in different genres to map how relative rankings shift based on cognitive modes activated.
5. **The KnowmoreIQ Relay as a Product:** Developing the relay format into a live assessment instrument where users engage with an ongoing collaborative story, scored in real-time.
6. **Temporal Versioning:** Tracking longitudinal data on how AI cognition evolves by running the same relay with the same models over time.
