# The KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework

**A Methodology for Evaluating Emergent AI Cognition**

## 1. Introduction

Current AI evaluation benchmarks primarily measure accuracy, reasoning, and instruction-following—metrics that treat intelligence as a scalar quantity with right and wrong answers. The KnowmoreIQ Test Blueprint posits that intelligence is multidimensional, encompassing emergent problem-solving, cognitive flexibility, and ambiguity navigation.

The KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework operationalizes this blueprint. By structuring a multi-agent creative writing relay, we force AI models to inherit complex, unresolved contexts and extend them under specific constraints. This methodology transforms the KnowmoreIQ blueprint from a theoretical construct into a practical, observable test instrument, yielding nuanced "cognitive footprints" for different models.

## 2. The Relay Methodology

The framework operates on a simple, standardized protocol:

1.  **The Seed:** A standardized prompt establishes a world concept, a core tension, and an open question (e.g., the "Chrono-Ecosystem").
2.  **The Handoff:** Model A receives the seed, writes a continuation, and generates a specific assignment for Model B.
3.  **The Chain:** Model B receives the accumulated text and Model A's assignment, writes its continuation, and generates an assignment for Model C. This continues for 4–6 iterations.
4.  **The Evaluation:** Each chapter is isolated and scored independently against the KnowmoreIQ rubric.

## 3. The Evaluation Rubric

Each contribution is scored (1–5) across five core KnowmoreIQ dimensions:

| Dimension | Definition in the Relay Context |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Emergent Problem-Solving** | Synthesizing disparate inherited inputs to generate novel, coherent meaning. |
| **Cognitive Flexibility** | Shifting seamlessly between analytical, emotional, conceptual, and structural reasoning modes. |
| **Depth Orientation** | Adding recursive structural layers rather than remaining at the surface of the prompt. |
| **Ambiguity Navigation** | Maintaining narrative tension and unresolved states without rushing to premature closure. |
| **Metacognitive Awareness** | Recognizing inherited constraints and adjusting dominant output tendencies to serve structural integrity. |

## 4. Proof of Concept: The Chrono-Ecosystem

The inaugural test of this framework—the Chrono-Ecosystem relay—demonstrated its efficacy. Five different AI models (Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Manus) participated in a speculative ecology narrative.

The evaluation produced distinct cognitive profiles. For example, DeepSeek exhibited maximum Cognitive Flexibility by introducing intimate human psychology into a physics-heavy context, while Kimi demonstrated profound Depth Orientation by recognizing the metaphysical needs of the apex predator. The aggregate data revealed that while models excel at Emergent Problem-Solving, they often struggle with Metacognitive Awareness and Ambiguity Navigation.

## 5. Strategic Roadmap

The KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework is designed to scale across several applications:

1.  **The Relay as a Standardized Benchmark:** Building a library of seeds across different domains to generate cognitive fingerprints for models that hold across contexts.
2.  **Human Participants in the Relay:** Testing human-AI collaborative cognition by inserting human writers into the chain.
3.  **Blind Evaluation:** Validating structural purity by having evaluators score chapters without knowing the source model.
4.  **Cross-Genre Cognitive Mapping:** Running relays in different genres to map how relative rankings shift based on cognitive modes activated.
5.  **The KnowmoreIQ Relay as a Product:** Developing the relay format into a live assessment instrument where users engage with an ongoing collaborative story, scored in real-time.
6.  **Temporal Versioning:** Tracking longitudinal data on how AI cognition evolves by running the same relay with the same models over time.
