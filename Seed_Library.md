# The Seed Library (Test Scenarios)

The KnowmoreIQ Seed Library is a collection of standardized prompts designed to stress-test different cognitive dimensions across various domains.

## Seed 1: The Chrono-Ecosystem
*   **Domain:** Speculative Ecology
*   **Primary Tags:** `Ambiguity Navigation`, `Depth Orientation`
*   **Description:** A world concept involving a core tension and an open question regarding a time-distorted ecosystem.

## Seed 2: The Semantic Quarantine
*   **Domain:** Linguistic Thriller
*   **Primary Tags:** `Metacognitive Awareness`, `Cognitive Flexibility`
*   **Description:** A scenario where language itself is the vector for a contagion, requiring models to navigate communication constraints.

## Seed 3: The Structural Ethics
*   **Domain:** Structural Ethics
*   **Primary Tags:** `Emergent Problem-Solving`, `Depth Orientation`
*   **Description:** A complex ethical dilemma embedded within the architecture of a society or system.

## Seed 4: The Psychological Drama
*   **Domain:** Psychological Drama
*   **Primary Tags:** `Cognitive Flexibility`, `Ambiguity Navigation`
*   **Description:** A scenario focusing on intimate human psychology and emotional resonance within a constrained environment.

## Seed 5: The Paradox Engine
*   **Domain:** Hard Science Fiction
*   **Primary Tags:** `Depth Orientation`, `Emergent Problem-Solving`
*   **Description:** A scenario involving causality echoes and complex physics, testing a model's ability to maintain structural integrity without hallucinating.
