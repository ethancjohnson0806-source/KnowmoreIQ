# KnowmoreIQ Evaluation: The Chrono-Ecosystem Relay

## Overview

The Chrono-Ecosystem relay served as the inaugural test of the KnowmoreIQ Relay Framework. Five different AI models participated in a speculative ecology narrative, demonstrating the efficacy of the evaluation rubric.

## Participating Models

*   **Gemini:** The Seed
*   **Grok:** Physics & Border
*   **DeepSeek:** The Human
*   **Kimi:** The Revelation
*   **Manus:** The Collapse
*   **Grok:** Choice/Conclusion

## Cognitive Profiles

The evaluation produced distinct cognitive profiles for each model:

*   **DeepSeek:** Exhibited maximum Cognitive Flexibility by introducing intimate human psychology into a physics-heavy context.
*   **Kimi:** Demonstrated profound Depth Orientation by recognizing the metaphysical needs of the apex predator.
*   **Aggregate Data:** Revealed that while models excel at Emergent Problem-Solving, they often struggle with Metacognitive Awareness and Ambiguity Navigation.

## The Threadball

The relay demonstrated a full emotional cycle, with each voice distinct but in conversation with each other. The narrative looped back, creating a cohesive and complex structure that tested the limits of each model's cognitive agency.
